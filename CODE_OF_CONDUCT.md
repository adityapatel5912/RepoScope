# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

We as members, contributors, and leaders pledge to make participation in the **RepoScope** community a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, caste, color, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

We pledge to act and interact in ways that contribute to an open, welcoming, diverse, inclusive, and healthy community.

---

## Our Standards

Examples of behavior that contributes to a positive environment for our community include:

* **Demonstrating empathy and kindness** toward other people.
* **Being respectful of differing opinions, viewpoints, and architectural approaches.**
* **Giving and gracefully accepting constructive technical feedback.**
* **Accepting responsibility and apologizing** to those affected by our mistakes, and learning from the experience.
* **Focusing on what is best** not just for us as individuals, but for the overall community, software reliability, and user safety.

Examples of unacceptable behavior include:

* The use of sexualized language or imagery, and sexual attention or advances of any kind.
* Trolling, insulting or derogatory comments, and personal or political attacks.
* Public or private harassment.
* Publishing others' private information, such as physical or email addresses, without their explicit permission.
* Dismissive or elitist comments regarding technical expertise or tooling choices.
* Other conduct which could reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional open-source setting.

---

## Enforcement Responsibilities

Community leaders are responsible for clarifying and enforcing our standards of acceptable behavior and will take appropriate and fair corrective action in response to any behavior that they deem inappropriate, threatening, offensive, or harmful.

Community leaders have the right and responsibility to remove, edit, or reject comments, commits, code, wiki edits, issues, and other contributions that are not aligned to this Code of Conduct, and will communicate reasons for moderation decisions when appropriate.

---

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies within all community spaces (including GitHub repositories, issue trackers, discussions, and pull requests), and also applies when an individual is officially representing the community in public spaces. Examples of representing our community include using an official email address, posting via an official social media account, or acting as an appointed representative at an online or offline event.

---

## Reporting & Enforcement

Instances of abusive, harassing, or otherwise unacceptable behavior may be reported privately to the project maintainer:

* **Maintainer**: Aditya Patel
* **GitHub Profile**: [@adityapatel5912](https://github.com/adityapatel5912)
* **Reporting Channel**: Contact options on GitHub profile or private maintainer email.

Please do **not** open a public issue for a conduct report.

All reports will be reviewed and investigated promptly and fairly. The maintainer will:
1. Acknowledge receipt of the report within 48 hours.
2. Review the evidence confidentially with the parties involved.
3. Take proportionate, fair corrective action (warning, content removal, or ban).
4. Respect the privacy and confidentiality of the reporter at all times.

---

## Enforcement Guidelines

Community leaders will follow these guidelines when determining consequences:

1. **Correction**: A private, written warning clarifying the violation and requesting behavioral change.
2. **Warning**: A formal warning with specific consequences for repeated behavior.
3. **Temporary Ban**: A temporary suspension from interacting with all RepoScope repositories, issues, and discussions.
4. **Permanent Ban**: A permanent ban from public interaction with the RepoScope project and community.
